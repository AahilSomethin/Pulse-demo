# On-screen headlines are separate. Do not add claims that are not in this script.

Across the Maldives, vessels move between islands every day. But understanding these connections from raw tracking data is challenging.

SeaPulse processes historical GPS observations to reveal patterns in maritime movement.

By reconstructing journeys and examining repeated movements, we can measure observed connections, journey times, and variability.

These measurements help identify connections with unusual patterns that transport planners can investigate further.

Authorised live tracking, ferry schedules, and passenger demand could later support predictive planning. Today, this prototype only shows how tracked vessels moved.
